### Featured Projects

**Predictive Modeling of High-Frequency Market Regimes** · 2026  
Hybrid ensemble for real-time financial decisions on anonymized big data. Feature engineering + PCA feeding a TensorFlow dense network combined with a PyTorch LSTM to capture temporal dependencies and reduce exposure during high-volatility regimes.  
`Python · TensorFlow · PyTorch · Scikit-Learn · PCA`

**Real-Time Suspicious Transaction Detection (AML)** · 2024–2025  
Anomaly detection over anonymized transactions, modeled as a Data Lake plus a risk Data Mart in BigQuery with optimized SQL for feature extraction. Root-cause analysis of false positives drove threshold tuning and typology-level KPIs, surfaced to compliance stakeholders through QuickSight.  
`BigQuery · Python · SQL · Apache Beam · QuickSight`

**Automated Credit Scoring & Expected Loss (IFRS 9)** · 2025  
SAS macros for cleaning, transformation and segmentation of large transactional databases, plus PD and LGD estimation models built for regulatory compliance and faster processing.  
`SAS Base · SAS/STAT · SAS Macro · SQL`

**Customer Retention via Survival Analysis** · 2025–2026  
Churn-pattern model over millions of historical records, estimating the retention curve and isolating key risk factors so the business team could time interventions.  
`SAS Enterprise Miner · PROC LIFETEST · PROC PHREG`

**Flight Status Prediction** · 2024  
Classification model over 60,000+ U.S. flight records predicting delays and cancellations.  
`Python · Scikit-learn · Pandas · SQL`
